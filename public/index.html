🏒⚾ Sports Schedule Calendar

A month-at-a-glance view of every team's schedule across five leagues. All start times are US Eastern.

The leagues

  • NHL — National Hockey League
  • AHL — American Hockey League
  • ECHL
  • NCAA — Division I men's hockey
  • MLB — incl. spring training & postseason

Two tabs

  • Calendar — the month view described here.
  • RoadTrip Planning — pick several teams and a number of days, and it finds every way to catch each of them at a home game on a separate day within that window. Tick “visit in this exact order” to fix the sequence.

Getting around (Calendar)

  • ‹ › move between months; Today jumps back to now.
  • Use the Leagues & Teams panel on the right to switch a whole league on/off, or expand one (▸) to pick individual teams. On a phone it's behind the Filters button.

Reading a day

  • Each game shows away @ home and the ET start time — or the score with F once it's final, LIVE while in progress.
  • A gold * means the home team has a promotion that night (giveaway, theme night, fireworks…). NHL & MLB only.
  • Click any game — or “+N more” — for full details: venue, exact ET time, and the promotion list.

🚗 Roadtrips

  • Build your own: in a game's details tick “Add this game to my roadtrip”; the Roadtrip button in the top bar then shows your picked games in date order with a Google Maps route between arenas.
  • Let it plan for you: the RoadTrip Planning tab takes 2+ teams and a day count and lists every itinerary that catches each one at a home game — add any result to your roadtrip with one click.

Data comes from each league's public feed; schedules for a not-yet-started season fill in as that league releases them. Open this again anytime with the ? button.
